## Working memory

Our working memory is what we utilize for short term tasks but everything has to pass through working memory to become long term memory. 

The point is if we give a lot of information to human brain in a short time, it cannot process it.

## Schema

Our brain is good at forming and finding patterns or structures that help us group pieces of information and understand how complex processes work. This is described as chunking, creating groups or sets of familiar concepts to help retrieve more information, more quickly. 

We use existing experience to make sense of new information, and the more experience we have, the more short cuts we have to draw on.

## Retrieval practice

Flash cards
Yes/ no questions 
Multiple choice questions 
Short answer questions
Free recall

Teaching others - friend or someone less experienced
Write the concept in your own words
Make a list of key facts

Trying to remember what your have learned. 

Best way to get something to long term memory. The more you repeat, the better the neutral pathways get.

## Spaced Practice 

Recalling information at different time and spacing out retrieval prescribe is more beneficial
