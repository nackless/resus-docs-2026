This is an article about getting your name on the GMC consultant register through the portfolio pathway. 

I'm not writing this because I have any doubts about the breadth of material that is already available, but because I want to understand how much is just enough and if too much is actually required. 

Also, I'm currently putting my application together and I thought why not share my journey as I go along. 

To give you some context, I have been in the UK for the last 8 years, working in the capacity of clinical fellow. I have FRCEM and I have now decided to put together my application for the Portfolio pathway.

My plan is to share each step with you. I'm going to work on my CV first.
