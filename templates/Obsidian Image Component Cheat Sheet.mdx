# Obsidian Image Formatting Cheat Sheet (Resus Docs)

This cheat sheet shows how to insert images into your notes for publication on Resus Docs.

---

## 1. Standard Markdown Image Tag (Simple Default)

```markdown
![Airway Diagram](/assets/rsi-algorithm-flowchart.svg)
```
*Renders automatically centered with rounded corners (`rounded-lg`) and responsive width.*

---

## 2. Advanced MDX `<Img />` Component Snippets

Import statement at top of your note:
```mdx
import Img from '../src/components/Img.astro';
```

### 📍 A. Centered Image (Default)
```mdx
<Img
  src="/assets/rsi-algorithm-flowchart.svg"
  alt="Emergency Airway Protocol Flowchart"
  caption="Figure 1: Complete Emergency RSI & FONA Rescue Protocol."
  align="center"
  width="lg"
  rounded="xl"
  shadow="lg"
/>
```

### 👈 B. Text Wrapped around Image on the Right (`align="right"`)
```mdx
<Img
  src="/assets/reason-ultrasound-echo.svg"
  alt="POCUS Echo View"
  caption="Cardiac standstill on subxiphoid view."
  align="right"
  width="sm"
  rounded="md"
  shadow="md"
/>
Your clinical prose text goes here... It will automatically float to the left of the image and wrap around smoothly.
```

### 👉 C. Text Wrapped around Image on the Left (`align="left"`)
```mdx
<Img
  src="/assets/reason-ultrasound-echo.svg"
  alt="POCUS Echo View"
  caption="Parasternal long axis view."
  align="left"
  width="md"
  rounded="lg"
  shadow="none"
/>
Your clinical prose text goes here... It will automatically float to the right of the image and wrap around smoothly.
```

### ↔️ D. Wide Breakout Banner (`align="wide"`)
```mdx
<Img
  src="/assets/reason-ultrasound-echo.svg"
  alt="Landmark Trial Subgroup Analysis"
  caption="Figure 2: REASON Trial Multicenter Data Subgroup Matrix."
  align="wide"
  width="full"
  rounded="xl"
  shadow="lg"
/>
```

### 📐 E. Custom Pixel / Percentage Widths
```mdx
<Img
  src="/assets/rsi-algorithm-flowchart.svg"
  alt="Custom Sized Image"
  align="center"
  width="450px"
  rounded="full"
/>
```

---

## 📁 3. Adding Images to your Obsidian Project

1. Drag & drop any photo or diagram into your Obsidian vault's `public/assets/` folder.
2. In your note, reference it as `/assets/filename.png` or `/assets/filename.jpg`.
3. When you push to GitHub, our **Automated Sharp Engine** will downscale it (>1920px) and generate a compressed WebP version to save storage on your mobile phone!
