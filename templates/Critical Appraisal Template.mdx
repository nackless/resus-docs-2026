## 1. PICO Analysis Framework

| PICO Element | Study Specifics |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Population (P)** | Target patient cohort, inclusion & exclusion criteria. |
| **Intervention (I)** | Primary treatment or diagnostic strategy evaluated. |
| **Comparator (C)** | Control group, placebo, or standard of care. |
| **Outcome (O)** | Primary & secondary clinical endpoints. |
